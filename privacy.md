# Scriptube Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

Scriptube is a Chrome extension that downloads the transcript of a YouTube video as a text file. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: nothing leaves your browser.

## Data we collect

None. Scriptube does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, or usage data. There are no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no servers operated by us.

## How the extension works

When you open Scriptube, it reads the address of your current tab only to pre-fill a YouTube link. When you click Fetch, the extension requests the video page and its captions directly from youtube.com. The transcript is processed entirely in your browser and is only saved when you choose to copy it or download it as a file to your own computer. Requests to youtube.com are subject to Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Permissions

**activeTab** reads the current tab's URL to pre-fill the video link. **downloads** saves the transcript as a .txt file and confirms when it's done. **declarativeNetRequest** and **youtube.com access** are used to fetch caption data from YouTube.
